Major Festival Celebrations

Vasantha Panchami

Date: Friday, 23 January 2026

A sacred festival dedicated to Goddess Saraswati and celebrated with special prayers seeking wisdom, learning, and knowledge.

Shyamala Navaratri Mahotsavam

A grand celebration dedicated to Goddess Shri Siddhi Dhatri Shyamala Devi, featuring special poojas, rituals, and festivities in her honor.

Navaratri Mahotsavam

Dates: 11 October 2026 - 19 October 2026

Nine days of devotion, special alankarams, poojas, cultural activities, and celebrations dedicated to the Divine Mother.

Vijayadashami

Date: Tuesday, 20 October 2026

Celebrated as the triumph of Dharma over Adharma, marking the culmination of Navaratri festivities.

Why Worship Shri Siddhi Dhatri Shyamala Devi?

📚 Wisdom

Seek divine blessings for knowledge, wisdom, and spiritual understanding. Devotees of Goddess Siddhi Dhatri Shyamala Devi often share experiences of receiving her grace, guidance, and blessings after performing poojas and offering heartfelt prayers.

🎓 Education

Pray for academic excellence, concentration, and success in learning.

🎤 Eloquence

Receive blessings for speech, communication, creativity, and the arts.

🌺 Prosperity

Invite abundance, harmony, well-being, and positive growth in life.

Devotee Guidelines

  • Arrive at least 15 minutes before scheduled poojas.
  • Traditional attire is encouraged.
  • Maintain silence and respect within temple premises.
  • Follow instructions provided by temple authorities.
  • Photography may be restricted in certain areas.
